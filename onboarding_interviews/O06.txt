Interviewer: Thanks for joining. What's your role with LabLoop?
O06: I'm the department admin, so I create accounts for new people across nine labs and deal with billing. At the start of term I might add forty people in two weeks.
Interviewer: How does adding forty people go?
O06: One at a time. There's no import. I get a spreadsheet from the graduate office and I type each person in by hand. Name, email, lab, grant code. It takes most of two days.
Interviewer: What would you want instead?
O06: Upload a spreadsheet. That's it. Every other system I use here can do that.
Interviewer: What happens with grant codes for new people?
O06: That's a mess. New students often don't know which grant they're on, so they leave it blank, and then at the end of the quarter I can't bill their usage properly. I'd love LabLoop to require a grant code when they book, picked from a list, not typed.
Interviewer: How do new users find LabLoop, from your point of view?
O06: I get a lot of emails from confused new people who can't book anything. I usually have to forward them to their lab manager or the core. It's not really my area, but they come to me because my name is on the welcome email.
Interviewer: What about when people leave?
O06: I don't get told when people leave, so their accounts stay active. Every year or so I do a clean-up, but it's guesswork.
Interviewer: If you could change one thing, what would it be?
O06: Spreadsheet import for new accounts. It would give me back two days every term.
Interviewer: Thank you, that's very helpful.
O06: No problem.
